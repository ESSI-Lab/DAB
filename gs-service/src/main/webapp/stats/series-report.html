Series report

For each time series of the source, the metadata is compared with the data cache content and with the outcome of the last update. The statuses are computed by the Data harvester task when it aligns the data cache with the metadata (at its start, after each metadata harvesting and at least daily) and are updated each time a series is updated; the overdue and stale statuses are always current. Series are sorted from the most severe status, then from the oldest latest observation.
